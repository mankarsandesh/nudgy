# Privacy Policy

**Little Nudges – Animated Reminders** (Chrome extension)

Last updated: 30 October 2026

## Summary

Little Nudges does not collect, send, share or sell any personal data. Everything you enter stays in your browser, on your own computer.

## What the extension stores

The extension saves the following with Chrome's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`). It stays on your device and is not synced to other devices:

- Your reminders: title, optional description, type and schedule
- Your settings: sound, system notifications and snooze length
- "Done today" counts for each reminder type, kept for about the last 60 days
- Scheduling details, such as when each reminder will next appear
- A reminder you were still editing, so you can pick up where you left off if the popup closes

None of this is ever sent to us or to anyone else.

## What the extension does not do

- No accounts, sign-in or payments
- No analytics, tracking, advertising or cookies
- No network requests: the extension does not contact any server
- No access to the websites you visit, your browsing history or page content
- No sharing or selling of data to third parties

## Permissions

- `storage`: save your reminders, settings and counts on your device
- `alarms`: show reminders at the times you choose
- `notifications`: show an optional system notification when a reminder appears

## Deleting your data

You can delete any reminder in the extension's popup. Removing the extension from Chrome deletes all the data it stored.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
